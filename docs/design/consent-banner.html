NurseDex consent banner, settled design

Settled with Dan in five design rounds on 13 October 2026 (milestone "Cookie consent banner", issues #1160 and #1162). Drawn over real NurseDex screenshots; the pages behind the banner are pictures, not part of the design.

Decisions

  1. A full width bar along the bottom of the screen. Chosen over a floating corner card and a slimmer strip: it is impossible to miss, and it never covers content in a corner. The page stays usable behind it, so ignoring it counts as no.
  2. The bar is filled with a pale sage (#dde7e1, the sage-wash token) under a sage top edge. Dan, on the first build in the page's own off white: "I feel like it blends into the page". Chosen over a teal top edge and a cream fill. The Privacy policy link is dark teal on it, because the site's teal measured 4.0 to 1 on the sage, under the 4.5 text needs; dark teal measures 6.1 to 1, the sentence 10.0 to 1, and the teal buttons 4.0 to 1 against the bar.
  3. Decline and Accept are identical teal buttons. Dan asked whether Accept could be teal and Decline quieter. California's consent rules require symmetry in choice and treat a more prominent Accept as a dark pattern, and a lopsided banner is what the wiretapping suits point to, so both use the site's standard teal button. Standing rule: a consent choice never gives one answer more visual weight than the other.
  4. Blog videos show their own preview image, served from NurseDex. Dan rejected a plain panel ("I want to show what the video looks like"). Our server fetches the preview image from YouTube or Vimeo and serves it from nursedex.com, so the reader's browser contacts neither until they press play.
  5. A teal play button over the preview, and no visible cookie note. The privacy policy carries the disclosure, and the button's accessible name says the video plays from YouTube or Vimeo.
  6. Copy: "We'd like to run analytics and session recording in your browser to see where NurseDex is confusing and fix it. None of it runs unless you accept." followed by a Privacy policy link.

What is the medium, not the design

The screenshots, the scaling captions and the hand drawn buttons are this file's way of showing the design. In the app the bar is a React component using the existing Button (default variant, large size), the page padding and nav offset come from CSS variables, and the preview image is a NurseDex route, not an embedded picture.
